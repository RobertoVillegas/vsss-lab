# Design

## Formation bottleneck

Replace the arithmetic mean with the geometric mean of active support and coverage qualities.
The result stays smooth, bounded, identity-free, and compatible with reduced rosters. Unlike the
arithmetic mean, it cannot report a team improvement when one responsibility improves enough to
hide another's regression.

The fresh configuration uses coefficient `0.10`. Goals remain global and the potential remains
terminal-zeroed discounted shaping.

## Curriculum eligibility

Phase advancement requires consecutive phase skill holdouts plus motion eligibility: idle spin
and `STOP` must be within their existing ceilings. Goal throughput remains part of the complete
behavior verdict and final checkpoint promotion, but is excluded from phase eligibility because
later teaching is required to build integrated match competence.

## Rollback

Revert ADR 0024 or use the prior configuration and code. No checkpoint or artifact is deleted.
