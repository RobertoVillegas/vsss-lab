# Balanced dynamic-role formation follow-up

## Why

The first dynamic-role run showed that arithmetic aggregation let good coverage conceal degraded
support, while short-match goal noise withheld the defensive curriculum despite passing phase
skills and healthy motion.

## Milestone and non-goals

This is an M24.3 evidence-driven correction. It does not change observations, actions, physical
identity, role ownership, terminal reward sharing, skill floors, or final promotion gates.

## What changes

- Make active support and coverage jointly constrain the formation potential.
- Reduce the formation coefficient for a fresh controlled run.
- Keep motion collapse out of later phases without using short-match goals as a teaching gate.
